# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct / 贡献者契约行为准则

**English** | [简体中文](#简体中文)

---

## English

### Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

### Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community and project quality

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, API keys, or account credentials, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

### Enforcement Responsibilities

Community maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

### Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, Code Reviews) and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

### Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers via GitHub repository security/maintainer contact channels. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All community maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

### Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, available at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.

## 简体中文

### 我们的承诺

作为社区成员、贡献者和维护者，我们承诺让所有人在参与本开源社区时都能获得免受骚扰的体验，无论其年龄、体型、显性或隐性残障、民族、生理性别特征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育背景、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、肤色、宗教信仰或性取向如何。

我们承诺以促进开放、友善、多元化、包容和健康社区的方式行事与互动。

### 我们的标准

有助于为社区创造积极环境的行为包括：

- 对他人表现出同理心与友善
- 尊重不同的技术观点、见解和经验
- 提出并坦诚接受建设性的技术反馈与代码审查意见
- 勇于承担责任，向因我们的失误而受影响的人致歉，并从中吸取教训
- 专注于对整个社区与项目工程质量最有利的决策，而不仅限于个人偏好

不可接受的行为包括：

- 使用带有性暗示的语言或图像，以及任何形式的性骚扰
- 恶意挑衅（Trolling）、侮辱性或贬损性评论，以及人身或政治攻击
- 公开或私下的骚扰行为
- 未经明确许可发布他人的隐私信息（例如物理地址、电子邮箱、API 密钥或账户凭据等）
- 在专业技术社区环境中合理认为不恰当的其他行为

### 维护职责

项目维护者负责阐明和执行可接受行为的标准，并将对他们认为不恰当、具有威胁性、冒犯性或有害的任何行为采取适当且公正的纠正措施。

项目维护者有权也有责任删除、编辑或拒绝与本行为准则不符的评论、提交（Commits）、代码、Wiki 编辑、Issue 和其他贡献，并在适当情况下说明处理决定的原因。

### 适用范围

本行为准则适用于所有项目社区空间（包括 GitHub Issues、Pull Requests、Discussions、代码审查等），并且当个人在公共场合正式代表本社区时同样适用。

### 举报与执行

滥用、骚扰或其他不可接受的行为可通过 GitHub 仓库维护者联系渠道进行报告。所有投诉都将得到及时、公正的审查与调查。

所有项目维护者均有义务尊重任何事件报告者的隐私与安全。

### 致谢与来源

本行为准则改编自 [贡献者契约 (Contributor Covenant)](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) 2.1 版本，原文参见 <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>。
